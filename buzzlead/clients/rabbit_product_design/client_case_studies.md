## ⚠️ CASE STUDY GAP IDENTIFIED

Per onboarding: "We have not been doing B2B sales so I don't really have any measurable proof. Client base pretty much all individuals."

**This is a significant gap.** Cold emails with specific case studies perform 2-3x better than those without proof points.

## WORKAROUND: Credibility Statements

Until B2B case studies are developed, use these proof points:

### Social Proof One-Liners

| Statement | Use When |
|-----------|----------|
| "Our engineers have brought 1,000+ products to market" | General credibility |
| "Our team averages 27 years of engineering experience" | Competing against junior teams |
| "We're the only US firm with in-house patent research and analysis" | Differentiation |
| "We reduce prototype iterations by 50% through DFM validation" | Addressing rework concerns |
| "Our prototypes are CNC machined from production materials, not 3D printed" | Quality positioning |
| "BBB A+ rated with 20 five-star reviews" | Trust building |

### Experience-Based Proof

- "We've worked with founders from Apple, Google, Amazon Labs, and Beats by Dre"
- "500+ combined years of product design and engineering experience"
- "20+ years using today's CAD tools—we're not learning on your project"

## RECOMMENDED: Build These Case Studies

### Priority 1: The "Burned by Another Firm" Save
Find a client who came to Rabbit after a bad experience elsewhere.
**Structure needed:**
- What firm failed them
- How much money/time was wasted
- What Rabbit did differently
- Final outcome (shipped product? cost savings? timeline?)

**One-liner template:** "We helped a [product type] founder who'd already spent $[X] elsewhere finally get to production in [Y] weeks."

### Priority 2: The "Speed" Story
Find a client with a tight deadline that Rabbit hit.
**Structure needed:**
- What was the deadline (trade show? funding milestone?)
- How tight was the timeline
- What Rabbit delivered
- Outcome

**One-liner template:** "We helped a [product type] company hit their [CES/launch] deadline with a working prototype in just [X] weeks."

### Priority 3: The "Manufacturing Success" Story
Find a client whose product is now in production.
**Structure needed:**
- Initial state (prototype? idea?)
- Manufacturing challenges solved
- Production outcome (units shipped? cost savings?)

**One-liner template:** "We took a [product type] from concept to production, reducing manufacturing costs by [X]%."

## INTERIM COPY PATTERNS

Until case studies are available, use these patterns:

### Pattern 1: Experience-Based
```
"We've helped bring 1,000+ products to market. Our engineers average 27 years experience—they've seen (and solved) pretty much every hardware challenge."
```

### Pattern 2: Process-Based
```
"Most design firms hand you CAD files that look great but fail in production. We validate for manufacturability at every stage—our clients see 50% fewer prototype iterations."
```

### Pattern 3: Anti-Agency
```
"No project managers, no junior designers padding your bill. You'll work directly with senior engineers who've shipped products for Apple, Google, and Amazon Labs."
```

### Pattern 4: Risk Reversal
```
"We're not the cheapest—but we're usually cheaper than starting over with a third firm because the first two didn't work out."
```
