## Case Study 1: ProductEVO

### Company Profile

- **Industry**: Manufacturing / Product Sourcing
- **Service**: Helps companies source and manufacture products overseas
- **Client Type**: B2B, typically post-funding startups or growing DTC brands

### The Challenge

ProductEVO relied on referrals and word-of-mouth. Pipeline was inconsistent. No systematic outreach to capture companies actively looking for manufacturing partners.

### The Buzzlead Approach

- **Trigger**: Companies that just raised funding (Series A/B) and would need to scale manufacturing
- **Targeting**: Founders and Operations leaders at funded startups
- **Angle**: Positioned as "the manufacturing partner that scales with you post-funding"

### Results

- **$90,000 profit** in 9 months
- Consistent pipeline of qualified manufacturing opportunities
- Reduced reliance on referral-based growth

### One-Liner for Emails

"We helped a manufacturing sourcing company generate $90K in profit in 9 months using cold email to target post-funding startups."

### Best Used For

- Companies with services tied to growth/funding events
- B2B services where timing matters
- Offers with clear ROI tied to client growth stage

### Campaign Angle Template

```
Trigger: Post-funding announcement (Crunchbase, Google News)
Pain: Need to scale operations quickly, don't have manufacturing partner
Hook: "Congrats on the raise. When [company like them] scaled production post-Series A, they..."
```

## Case Study 2: DiamondLinks

### Company Profile

- **Industry**: SEO / Link Building Services
- **Service**: White-label link building and ORM for agencies
- **Client Type**: Digital marketing agencies, SEO agencies

### The Challenge

DiamondLinks relied heavily on customer referrals and inbound. Couldn't predictably forecast sales. Wanted outbound pipeline to agency partners.

### The Buzzlead Approach

- **Trigger**: Agencies actively doing SEO work (visible from case studies, client roster)
- **Targeting**: Agency owners and Directors of SEO
- **Angle**: "Competitive warfare" - positioned as weapons to destroy client competitors, not just "link building services"

### Results

- **$100,000+ in 5 weeks** (including $100K ARR reseller partnership)
- **10+ outbound sales meetings per month**
- Signed major reseller partner in first 5 weeks

### One-Liner for Emails

"We helped an SEO agency sign a $100K ARR reseller partnership in 5 weeks using competitive warfare positioning in cold email."

### Best Used For

- Agencies selling to other agencies (B2B2B)
- Services in competitive/saturated markets
- Offers that can be framed as "weapons" or strategic advantage

### Campaign Angle Template

```
Trigger: Agency winning new clients, expanding team (LinkedIn, job posts)
Pain: Need reliable white-label partner to scale without hiring
Hook: "When agencies like [competitor] need to scale link building without the overhead..."
```

## Case Study 3: Forever Fierce

### Company Profile

- **Industry**: Fitness Apparel / Gym Equipment
- **Service**: Custom branded apparel and equipment for gyms
- **Client Type**: Gym owners, fitness studio owners

### The Challenge

Forever Fierce relied on partners and referrals. Experimented with paid ads but costs were too high. Lead flow was inconsistent.

### The Buzzlead Approach

- **Trigger**: Gyms with strong brand presence (visible from social, website)
- **Targeting**: Gym owners and operations managers
- **Angle**: PROFIT-first positioning - "add $3K/yr profit to your business" not "buy apparel from us"
- **Method**: Data-scraped biggest gyms in target cities

### Results

- **125 meetings booked** in 4 months
- **$75,000+ in revenue** (not including $20K client signed after)
- **25+ qualified sales opportunities** per month on autopilot

### One-Liner for Emails

"We helped a gym apparel company book 125 meetings in 4 months by positioning their product as profit-add, not expense."

### Best Used For

- Products typically seen as expenses (reframe as profit)
- Local/regional B2B businesses
- Offers with clear ROI math ($X investment = $Y return)

### Campaign Angle Template

```
Trigger: Gym with strong brand, social presence, multiple locations
Pain: Always looking for ways to increase per-member revenue
Hook: "If we could add $3K/yr profit to [gym name] just like we did for Gym Launch..."
```

## One-Liner Templates

Use these patterns for third-party proof in emails:

**Result-First Pattern:**
"We helped [similar company type] [achieve specific result] in [timeframe] using [method]."

Examples:

- "We helped a manufacturing company generate $90K profit in 9 months targeting post-funding startups."
- "We helped an SEO agency sign a $100K reseller deal in 5 weeks using competitive positioning."
- "We helped a gym apparel brand book 125 meetings in 4 months by reframing their product as profit."

**Problem-First Pattern:**
"When [similar company] was struggling with [problem], we helped them [result]."

Examples:

- "When DiamondLinks couldn't forecast sales from referrals, we built them 10+ monthly outbound meetings."
- "When Forever Fierce's paid ads weren't working, we helped them book 25+ meetings per month."
