# Buzzlead Copywriting Templates

## Purpose

Email copy templates and frameworks for cold email campaigns. Used by Agent 5 (Copy Generator) but referenced here for Agent 2 campaign planning.

Last Updated: December 2024

---

## Email Framework: The 4-Part Cold Email

### Structure

1. **Hook** (1 line) - Pattern interrupt or relevant observation
2. **Context** (1-2 lines) - Why reaching out, trigger or pain
3. **Proof** (1 line) - Third-party credibility (case study)
4. **CTA** (1 line) - Soft ask, low friction

### Word Count Target

- **Total**: 50-80 words
- **Subject**: 3-6 words
- **Opening**: Under 15 words

---

## Template 1: Trigger-Based

**Use when:** Prospect has a recent trigger event (funding, hiring, new role, etc.)

```
Subject: [Trigger event] + [your angle]

Hey {first_name},

Saw [trigger event] - congrats/noticed/makes sense.

When [similar company] was at this stage, they [problem you solve]. We helped them [specific result].

Worth a quick chat to see if relevant for {company}?

{signature}
```

**Example:**
```
Subject: Post-Series B manufacturing

Hey Sarah,

Saw the Series B announcement - congrats on the raise.

When DTC brands hit this stage, scaling manufacturing becomes the bottleneck. We helped a similar brand cut production costs 40% in 3 months.

Worth a quick chat to see if relevant for Acme?

Best,
Nick
```

## Template 2: Pain-Based

**Use when:** Targeting a known pain point without specific trigger

```
Subject: Quick question about [pain area]

Hey {first_name},

Most [title/role] at [company type] tell us [pain point] is one of their biggest challenges.

When [similar company] faced this, we helped them [specific result] in [timeframe].

Is this on your radar at {company}?

{signature}
```

**Example:**
```
Subject: Quick question about link building

Hey Mike,

Most agency owners tell us scaling link building without compromising quality is their biggest headache.

When DiamondLinks faced this, we helped them 3x their capacity without hiring - they closed a $100K reseller deal in 5 weeks.

Is this on your radar at Growth Agency?

Best,
Nick
```

## Template 3: Observation-Based

**Use when:** You noticed something specific about their business

```
Subject: Noticed [observation]

Hey {first_name},

Was checking out {company} and noticed [specific observation about their business].

[Companies like them] usually [pain point related to observation]. We helped [similar company] [result].

Curious if you're exploring solutions for this?

{signature}
```

**Example:**
```
Subject: Noticed the new gym location

Hey Jessica,

Was checking out FitLife and noticed you just opened your third location - nice growth.

When gyms expand, branded merch usually gets deprioritized but it's actually a profit center. We helped Gym Launch add $3K/yr profit per location with custom apparel.

Curious if you're exploring this?

Best,
Nick
```

## Template 4: Mutual Connection / Social Proof

**Use when:** You have shared connection or they follow someone relevant

```
Subject: [Mutual connection] mentioned [topic]

Hey {first_name},

[Mutual connection] mentioned you're focused on [area].

We helped them [result] and thought you might find it relevant given {company}'s [observation].

Open to a quick chat?

{signature}
```

## Template 5: Case Study Direct

**Use when:** You have a highly relevant case study for their exact situation

```
Subject: How [similar company] [achieved result]

Hey {first_name},

Just helped [similar company] [achieve specific result] in [timeframe].

Given {company} is [similarity/trigger], thought you might find the approach interesting.

Want me to share what worked?

{signature}
```

## Subject Line Formulas

### High-Performing Patterns

1. **Question + Specificity**: "Quick question about [their specific thing]"
2. **Trigger + Angle**: "[Their news/event] + [your topic]"
3. **Result Hint**: "How [similar company] [did thing]"
4. **Observation**: "Noticed [specific thing]"
5. **Personalized**: "[First name] - [topic]"

### Avoid

- Generic subjects: "Quick sync", "Checking in", "Following up"
- Clickbait that doesn't match content
- ALL CAPS or excessive punctuation
- Overly long (keep under 6 words)

## Opening Line Formulas

### Do

- Reference something specific to them
- Acknowledge a trigger or achievement
- Ask about a specific challenge

### Don't

- "Hope this finds you well"
- "I know you're busy but..."
- "My name is [X] and I work at [Y]"
- "I wanted to reach out because..."

---

## CTA Formulas

### Soft Ask (Preferred)

- "Worth a quick chat to see if relevant?"
- "Is this on your radar?"
- "Open to exploring this?"
- "Curious if you're tackling this?"

### Medium Ask

- "Got 15 min this week?"
- "Would a quick call make sense?"

### Hard Ask (Use Sparingly)

- "Here's my calendar: [link]"
- "How's Tuesday at 2pm?"

---

## Proof Point Insertion

### Format

"We helped [similar company type] [achieve specific result] in [timeframe]."

### Examples

- "We helped a DTC brand cut manufacturing costs 40% in 3 months."
- "We helped an SEO agency sign a $100K reseller deal in 5 weeks."
- "We helped a gym apparel company book 125 meetings in 4 months."

### Rules

1. Always use specific numbers (not "improved" or "increased")
2. Include timeframe when impressive
3. Match proof to prospect's industry/situation
4. Keep to one sentence

## Follow-Up Sequence

### Timing

- Follow-up 1: 2-3 days after initial
- Follow-up 2: 4-5 days after FU1
- Follow-up 3: 5-7 days after FU2 (optional)

### Follow-Up Templates

**FU1 - Add Value:**
```
Hey {first_name},

Following up on my note about [topic].

One thing I didn't mention - [additional insight or value add].

Still relevant?

{signature}
```

**FU2 - Breakup:**
```
Hey {first_name},

Circling back one more time on [topic].

If timing isn't right, no worries. Just let me know and I'll check back in a few months.

{signature}
```

**FU3 - Humor (Optional):**
```
Hey {first_name},

I promise this is my last email about [topic].

Either my timing is off, or I'm not explaining the value well. Happy to try again if you're curious.

{signature}
```

## Personalization Variables

### Required

- `{first_name}` - Prospect first name
- `{company}` - Prospect company name

### Recommended

- `{trigger}` - Recent trigger event
- `{observation}` - Something specific about them
- `{similar_company}` - Relevant case study company
- `{result}` - Relevant case study result

### Advanced

- `{mutual_connection}` - Shared connection
- `{technology}` - Tech they use
- `{recent_post}` - Their recent LinkedIn post
- `{job_post}` - Their job posting

## Tone Guidelines

### Do

- Write like a peer, not a vendor
- Be direct and specific
- Show you did research
- Be conversational
- Use short sentences

### Don't

- Use corporate speak
- Over-explain
- Be overly formal
- Use jargon
- Write paragraphs

### Voice Check

Read aloud. If it sounds like marketing copy, rewrite.
Should sound like a founder texting another founder.
